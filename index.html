About Me

Hello! I'm Jack, a games programmer, website developer and designer from the UK. I started working part time in a small IT company at age 13 during the first pandemic. For the next couple of years I would continue to build computers while also doing my GCSEs.

After entering Sixth Form (College in the UK), I branched out and started working on developing websites for my employer, whilst also studying Games Development and Design at College. Currently I am a BSc(Hons) Creative Computing student at the University of the Arts London
