Accessible Version of About Me

Hello! I'm Jack, a games programmer and website developer & designer from the UK. I started working part time in a small IT company at age 13 during the first pandemic. Within the first week I had built a complete and working PC. For the next couple of years I would continue to build computers while also doing my GCSEs. After entering Sixth Form (College in the UK), I branched out and started working on developing websites for my employer, whilst also studying Games Development and Design at College. Currently I am a BSc(Hons) Creative Computing student at the University of the Arts London This site you're on now is a showcase of both my web skills and a compilation of my previous projects. Some of the projects displayed have been completed, but may have changes made to them over time.
